Blog for notes, post-mortems, and experiments from the projects page. Two rules: every post ends with the measured result, and nothing is evergreen — wrong posts get rewritten.

## What to expect

- **Post-mortems** of projects that shipped — and one or two that didn't
- **Notes** from the ML/bioinformatics work I'm doing in my final year
- **Measured results only**: if I can't benchmark it, it doesn't get posted

To add a post, just drop a markdown file with frontmatter (`title`, `date`, `tags`, `excerpt`) into `src/content/blog/` and commit.
